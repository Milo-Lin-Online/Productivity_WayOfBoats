🏆 How the World Cup works
• Tap a category's circle each day you do it — that builds your 🔥 streak.
• Miss a day and that streak resets to 0, but the check-in still counts toward your month.
• Every check-in this month earns leaderboard points, consecutive or not: your month total ÷ 10. Go to the gym 19 scattered days and that's 1.9 points.
• Keep a category checked 26 days in one month and you catch 3 🎣 fish (once per category, per month).
• Days roll over at 1:00 AM Eastern Time (a little grace for night owls).
• Everyone sets their own categories — tap "⚙️ my goals" on your card.
